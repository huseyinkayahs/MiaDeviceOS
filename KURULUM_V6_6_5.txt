1. ZIP dosyasini proje ana klasorunun uzerine cikarin.
2. CMD ile deployment\production klasorune gecin.
3. Calistirin:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\repair-production-entry.ps1"
4. Kontrol edin:
   docker compose --env-file .env.production -f docker-compose.production.yml ps
5. Tarayicida ana adresi acin. Otomatik /admin.html sayfasina yonlenmelidir.

NOT: docker compose down -v kullanmayin.
