FACTORYBOX ONE v6.7.0 - CLOUD ACCESS & REMOTE MANAGEMENT

1) Backend'i / production servislerini durdurmayin; ZIP dosyasini proje veya C:\FactoryBox klasorunun uzerine kopyalayin.
2) Production klasorunde yonetici PowerShell acin.
3) Uygulama guncellemesi:

   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\apply-v6.7.0.ps1"

4) Admin panelde Ctrl+F5 yapin ve v6.7.0 kontrol edin.
5) System Settings > Remote Access & Domain ekranini acin.

GECICI INTERNET TESTI:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\start-quick-remote-access.ps1"

KALICI DOMAIN:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\enable-cloudflare-tunnel.ps1" -TunnelToken "TOKEN" -PublicUrl "https://panel.example.com" -AccessProtected

UZAK ERISIMI KAPATMA:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\disable-remote-access.ps1"

NOT: Quick Tunnel test amaclidir. Production icin named tunnel + Cloudflare Access kullanin.
